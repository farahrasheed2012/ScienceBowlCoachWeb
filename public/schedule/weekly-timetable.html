  • Exercise: 15 min daily — wait 30 min after any eating before exercising.
  • 2:00–3:00 PM is always free time — nothing gets scheduled in that block.

  • Dinner is at 7:00 PM every day.
  • Study blocks: Mon–Thu = 30 min Algebra + 30 min science · Friday = science only — 3:45–4:15 is a free block.
  • Clarinet after eating? Always leave 20–30 min between a meal and clarinet.
